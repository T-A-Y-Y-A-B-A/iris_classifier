SUMMARY

Full Pipeline Walkthrough

01Load & Explore
Import load_iris() from sklearn, wrap in a DataFrame, inspect shape, class balance, and statistical summary. Confirm 150 balanced samples across 3 classes.
02Split → Scale
Use train_test_split(test_size=0.2, shuffle=True) for 120 training / 30 test. Fit StandardScaler only on training data, then transform both sets to prevent data leakage.
03Find Best K (Elbow Method)
Loop K from 1 to 20, record train/test accuracy. Pick K at the lowest test error before it starts rising. Use KNeighborsClassifier(n_neighbors=best_k).
04Train & Predict
Call model.fit(X_train, y_train) to memorize the training map. Then model.predict(X_test) to classify unseen samples by majority vote among K nearest neighbors.
05Evaluate with F1 + Confusion Matrix
Use confusion_matrix() and classification_report(). Accuracy alone can be misleading on imbalanced data — F1 score balances precision and recall per class.
